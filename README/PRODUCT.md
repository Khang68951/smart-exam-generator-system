# Product

## Register

product

## Users
- **Students**: Users seeking targeted, curriculum-aligned practice exams based on specific study materials. They need a distraction-free, highly readable reading and testing environment.
- **Educators**: Users configuring and reviewing assessment criteria. They need precise tools to verify context boundaries, edit generated prompts, and export the output.

## Product Purpose
SEGS (Strict Context-Bounding Exam Generation System) transforms raw curriculum guidelines, lecture notes, and textbook passages into interactive exams. It eliminates AI hallucination entirely by strictly bounding generation to the uploaded context and providing paragraph-level citations for every question.

## Brand Personality
- **Precise**: Grounded in strict evidence, error-free, and unambiguous.
- **Academic & Professional**: Dignified, calm, and focused on study without gamified distraction.
- **Trustworthy**: Secure, reliable, and visually clear.

## Anti-references
- **SaaS Clichés**: Over-reliance on generic purple-to-pink gradients, glassmorphism as a default, and nested cards.
- **AI Grammar**: Saturated decorative accents, tiny tracked uppercase kickers above every element, and arbitrary numbered section labels (01, 02, etc.).
- **Unreadable Typography**: Low-contrast gray body text, line lengths extending beyond 75ch, or cramped display headings.

## Design Principles
- **Evidence-First Hierarchy**: Always display source materials alongside generated questions, prioritizing clear cross-referencing and interactive visual link highlights.
- **Readability Over Decoration**: Use premium fonts, generous line spacing, and optimal container widths (65–75ch for text) to ensure comfortable reading.
- **Purposeful Interaction**: Animations must reinforce state changes (e.g., role switching, generation loading) and must never be used purely for decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **Contrast**: Maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for body copy and 3:1 for display headings.
- **Motion**: Fully support `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` to disable transitions for sensitive users.
